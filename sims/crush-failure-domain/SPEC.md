# crush-failure-domain: spec

One concept: **same data, rule by host vs by rack; then lose a rack.** Task `docs/tasks/w3/crush-failure-domain.md`,
catalogue `sto-crush-failure-domain` (§4.6). Class stepper. Builds on `ceph-min-size` (PG states and colours).

## Scene

- **Pool line:** the pool's name, `size · min_size` (or `EC 4+2 · min_size 4`), the PG count, and the health word
  (`HEALTH_OK` solid with ✓, `HEALTH_WARN` dashed with ⚠) once the PGs are placed.
- **The rule** as CRUSH map text (`rule replicated_rule { … step chooseleaf firstn 0 type host … }`; `indep` for
  the EC pool), the failure-domain word outlined; on the highlight in the step that changed it.
- **The CRUSH tree:** `root default` over three rack frames (rack1–3, the frame in the rack's series colour), three
  host boxes each (ceph-r1-1 … ceph-r3-3, 12 OSDs each). A lost rack: dashed `--danger` frame crossed, "✕ lost".
- **Totals card:** the four state counts for every PG (the changed ones on the highlight), the risk line ("! n of N
  PGs have two or three copies in one rack", from step 2), holes when there are any. In the totals step: the
  comparison table (rule, PGs at risk, health before, and after the loss: stopped / degraded / clean).
- **Sample strip** (K3 block grid): PGs 0–11, one row each, one cell per copy (text = host, `r1-3` for ceph-r1-3;
  colour = rack), drawn grouped by rack; a rack holding too many of a PG's copies outlined (a span) and named in the
  aside ("! rack1"); copies in the lost rack grey crossed (`dead`); holes dashed (`ghost`, "NONE"). The aside ends with
  the PG's state word after a loss (or when it is not clean), on the highlight when it changed in this step.
- **Every PG** (`showAll`): a bitmap of every PG of the pool by state (clean solid, degraded hatched, inactive a hollow
  red frame, no copy grey with a diagonal); before a loss, a PG one rack can stop has a bar under it. Drawn in the
  page, not with K3 (NOTES.md).

## State

`{ pool, rule, lostRack: null | 1..3, down: [osd], placed, risk, pgs: 'cdis…', counts: { clean, degraded, inactive,
stale }, risky, holes, health, sample: [pg], ev, i }`. `pgs` is one letter per PG (c clean, d degraded, i inactive,
s stale), so the state stays small. `ev`: `place` / `risk` `{ rule, switched, back, placedNow }`, `lose` `{ rack, hit,
changed (sample PGs only), nChanged, alt? }`, `totals` `{ totals: [per rule], lost }`.

The raw CRUSH output is not in the state: it is constant per pool and rule (`placement.js`, `rawOf(pool, rule)`).

## Placement

`placement.js` is generated by `tools/gen-placement.mjs` with the cephsim engine (`sims/cephsim/js`: `compile.js`
`compileMap`, `engine/pgmap.js` `mapPool`, `engine/crush.js` straw2 `chooseleaf`, HASHPSPOOL) on cephsim's own sample
documents (`samples.js` `makeSample('brokenRule')` and `makeSample('threeRacks')`), with the one rule replaced by
`chooseleaf firstn|indep 0 type host|rack` (no device class).

- **Hardware** (cephsim `threeRacks`): rack1–3 × hosts ceph-r<r>-1…3 × 12 OSDs (10 hdd 8 TiB + 2 nvme 2 TiB), numbered
  in order: osd.0–35 in rack1, 36–71 in rack2, 72–107 in rack3. The generator checks the numbering.
- **rbd** (cephsim `brokenRule`): pool 1, replicated, size 3, min_size 2, 2048 PGs, `firstn`.
- **ec** (cephsim `threeRacks` `cephfs_data`): pool 3, erasure 4+2, min_size 4, 1024 PGs, `indep`.

## Rules

Sources: cephsim `js/engine/crush.js` (chooseleaf firstn / indep), `js/engine/pgmap.js` (`pgState`, `evaluatePool`);
`sims/ceph-min-size/SPEC.md` rules 1–4; Ceph documentation "CRUSH Maps" (failure domains, `chooseleaf`) *(check)*.

1. `chooseleaf … type X` places each copy under a **different** bucket of type X. With `type host` the three copies
   are on three hosts, and two may share a rack (rbd: **1390 of 2048** PGs have two or three copies in one rack);
   with `type rack` never (0).
2. A rule cannot place more copies than there are buckets of its type. Three racks → at most three copies under
   `type rack`; the rest are holes (`NONE`), and the PG is undersized from birth. EC 4+2 by rack: every one of the
   1024 PGs has three shards and three holes, so 3 < min_size 4: **inactive before anything fails**. rbd size 3 by rack
   has no holes.
3. PG states after a loss follow `ceph-min-size` rules 1–4 (its `group`, copied with its source line): live copies ≥
   min_size → active (all of `size`: clean, fewer: degraded); fewer than min_size → inactive; none → no copy. For size
   3 / min_size 2: ≥ 2 copies in the lost rack → inactive, exactly 1 → degraded, 0 → clean. A hole counts as a missing
   copy. **At risk** (the "!" and the risk count): a rack holds more than `width − min_size` of the PG's copies (rbd:
   two or three; EC 4+2 / min_size 4: three or more), which is exactly "losing that rack stops this PG" (tested for
   every PG, rack and rule).
4. All copies in the lost rack → no copy (Ceph: `stale`); if the rack is destroyed rather than powered off, that data
   is gone. rbd by host, rack 1: **33** PGs.
5. Before the loss both rules report `HEALTH_OK` (rbd): the rule's weakness is invisible until the failure (cephsim
   sample comment). After a loss `HEALTH_WARN`: down OSDs, inactive and degraded PGs are warnings in Ceph *(check;
   cephsim's `health.js` reports inactive PGs as HEALTH_ERR, see NOTES.md)*. EC by rack is not OK even before.
6. **Tie-breaks.** The sample strip is PGs 0–11 in id order; totals always count every PG. Copies are drawn grouped
   by rack (1, 2, 3), in CRUSH order within a rack, holes last (so the copies one rack shares stand side by side and
   one span outlines them). A rule switch in the comparison brings the lost rack back first and places afresh (the
   page does not model data moving). `lostRack` and `compare` from the URL (strings) normalise to the same values.

The page shows the groups as words (clean, degraded, inactive, no copy), as `ceph-min-size`'s grids do, not Ceph's
state strings; the task's `incomplete+inactive` / `stale+inactive` are cephsim's names (`ceph-min-size` NOTES.md,
deviations).

## Steps

One rule: (1) **place** — the placement under the rule, health; (2) **risk** — the racks holding too many copies are
outlined, the risk count; (3) **lose** — the rack goes down, every PG's state recomputed (only with a lost rack).
Comparison (`compare`) with a lost rack: 1–3 under the rule, 4–6 under the other rule (the rack back first), (7)
**totals** side by side. Comparison with nothing lost: (1) the rule placed with its risk, (2) the other rule with its
risk and the totals.

| preset | params | steps |
|---|---|---|
| `by-host-lose-rack` | lostRack 1 | 3 |
| `by-rack-lose-rack` | rule rack, lostRack 1 | 3 |
| `before-the-loss` | compare | 2 |
| `ec-by-host` | pool ec, lostRack 1 | 3 |
| `host-then-rack` | compare, lostRack 1 | 7 |
| (defaults) | rule host, nothing lost | 2 |

## Params

`pool` rbd / ec (rbd); `rule` host / rack (host); `lostRack` none / 1 / 2 / 3 (none); `compare` (off); `showAll`
(off, a view only: it changes neither steps nor states). Every param but `showAll` resets.
